# Results index

This index is canonical English and links to frozen machine-readable artifacts instead of recreating a second result database. The compact table is [`assets/final_results_summary.csv`](assets/final_results_summary.csv).

## Primary physiological replication — COMPLETE / FROZEN

The completed PhysioNet cohort report describes 12–13 Hz task-versus-rest reductions at central C3/C4 during fists imagery. For the replication 2–109 cohort, the frozen result table records negative participant estimates in 87/105 at C4 and 91/105 at C3. Read [full-cohort replication](full_cohort_replication.md) for definitions, uncertainty, correction, exclusions, and figures.

## Decoding — COMPLETE / FROZEN

Participant-specific CSP+LDA had median balanced accuracy 0.658 across the 86 eligible held-out-run evaluation participants. Strict zero-shot CSP had median 0.570 in its different cross-participant design. These values are not directly interchangeable with an online BCI benchmark. See [within-subject](within_subject_decoding.md) and [cross-subject](cross_subject_decoding.md).

## Secondary results — COMPLETE

Individualized-mu reliability, calibration quantity/diversity, spatial personalization, domain/direction alignment, and Riemannian decoding are deliberately retained as secondary evidence. They show heterogeneous performance and do not justify a universal decoder claim. The [final integrated report](final_scientific_report.md) summarizes their interpretation.
