# Limitations

The completed results concern one public, historical motor-imagery corpus and explicit classical analysis pipelines. They are sensor-level associations: scalp EEG cannot establish individual-neuron activity, causal mechanisms, or clinical utility.

- The data are offline and do not demonstrate real-time BCI control, user experience, safety, or clinical benefit.
- Limited runs/trials and repeated measures constrain inference; subject heterogeneity is an empirical result, not a reason to remove low-performing people.
- Fixed-band and individualized-peak analyses have different uncertainty profiles; an unavailable or unstable peak is legitimate missing information, not a value to force.
- Cross-subject and calibration findings depend on the frozen split, features, and participant cohort. They do not establish universal zero-shot transfer.
- The completed corpus does not establish day/session or laboratory/hardware generalization. Lee2019 is designed to address part of that gap, but is still **IN PROGRESS**.
- Later mechanistic and adaptation analyses are secondary; they provide useful constraints but should not be read as new confirmatory physiology.
- Raw-data licensing and provider terms remain external obligations.

The [final scientific report](final_scientific_report.md) gives study-specific interpretation boundaries.
